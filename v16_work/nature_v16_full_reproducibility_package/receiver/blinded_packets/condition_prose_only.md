# Background material (condition P: narrative only)

Reasoning systems can look competent locally while losing track of distinctions
that matter globally. When a system compresses its internal state into a simple
summary, two situations that should be treated differently can end up looking
the same, and then downstream judgements about them are shared. Adding the
ability to talk about one's own reasoning can help notice problems, though it
does not by itself give the system a genuinely new general capability. When a
system's workload keeps growing, its backlog of unresolved issues can keep
growing too, unless it gains a new general way of working or scales up how much
it can handle. Some correspondences to the physical world and to civilization
scale have been proposed as possibilities. Different jobs a mind does — taking
things in, recalling, local work, and broad integration — feel qualitatively
different from each other.
