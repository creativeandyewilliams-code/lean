# Formalization scope and scientific burden

The project is not intended to formalize every sentence in the paper. It targets every load-bearing theorem family required to distinguish a mathematical possibility theorem from a registry or simulation conformance claim.

## Semantic foundation

`Core/Semantics.lean` formalizes an admissible metric-interaction isomorphism and a target language generated from node type, edge type, incidence, metric, boundary, certificate, warrant, continuation, fitness, Boolean connectives, and bounded node/edge quantification. `Formula.eval_map_iff` is the formal semantic-equivalence theorem.

## Representation ontology

`Reflective/Subspace.lean` gives a constructive normalization of all registered roles to node, process, or both. This theorem is intentionally a representation theorem over the declared registered language.

## Projection and conflation

`Projection/Conflation.lean` formalizes target factorization, fibre adequacy, the surjective factorization theorem, first-order conflation, local stability, and reflective necessity.

## Operation identity and topology

`FMI/Operations.lean` gives typed operations and proves Register/Recall nonreduction by domain obstruction. `FMI/Topology.lean` defines target-relative local/global support and parallel/sequential schedule, and makes transition-generated reachability explicit.

## Cognitive order

`Order/Composition.lean` replaces the invalid list-length proxy with a finite set of canonical class IDs and an actual equivalence. `orderInvariance` proves cardinality preservation under the equivalence. `orderIncrement` remains explicitly definitional.

## Fragmentation

`Fragmentation/Backlog.lean` derives growth from the queue recursion and arrival-service gap. `Regional.lean` proves a restricted theorem: internal regional certification can coexist with arbitrarily large unresolved required cross-region burden. `SemanticLoss.lean` supplies a decision-loss lower bound for collapsed target-different pairs.

## CNS

`CNS/Lift.lean` composes:

- a fresh qualifying composition class;
- preservation of prior classes;
- strict operand-domain expansion;
- bounded cumulative residual over a declared interval;
- a target-different pair not factorizable through the old projection.

The theorem conclusion contains all four results. `NearSingular.lean` formalizes the comparator gap. `Dynamic.lean` formalizes the conditional bridge and proves uniqueness of fixed points under contraction.

## Governance and propagation

The option theorem proves the manuscript's `2ε + δ` bound. The propagation module distinguishes the complete regenerative cascade from exposure and gives a six-stage multiplicative lower bound.

## Great Filter

The necessary-property theorem is not merely a list. Each property has an independent escape-witness premise, and sufficiency rules out that witness. The conditional theorem concludes the universal target outcome by composing explicit external coverage and outcome implications.

## CST

The CST modules formalize discrete transport and least consequence closure. Holonomy drift is a constructive changed-state witness. Hard admissibility remains a proposition prior to action ranking.

## External boundary

No Lean theorem proves that physical forces, present AI systems, historical innovations, civilizations, hazards, or astronomical residues instantiate the formal premises. Those remain external theorem parameters and evidence tracks.
